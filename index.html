Dark Atlas Uploader

Dark Atlas Uploader is a small private tool used only by the creator of the YouTube channel Dark Atlas (animated explainer videos about the Stone Age).

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, schedule their publication time and read the channel's own basic statistics. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.
